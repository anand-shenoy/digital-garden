# Converting a Jack-and-Jill Bathroom Into Two En-Suites

**Type:** Design solution
**Project:** GRG

## The Constraint
Client wanted to convert a 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom house into a master en-suite plus 4 bedrooms, all with their own en-suite. A large laundry area was also needed, on top of that.

## The Idea
Re-jigged the layout and used the existing jack-and-jill bathroom concept to add en-suites to two of the guest rooms, rather than building two entirely new bathrooms from scratch. This freed up enough space to fit a proper laundry area as well, which wasn't part of the original brief but became possible once the bathroom layout was solved this way.

## Why It Worked
[[jack-and-jill-bathrooms]] as a layout trick usually gets treated as a compromise, shared access between two rooms, a downside to design around. Here it became the enabling move for adding en-suites without a bigger footprint.
