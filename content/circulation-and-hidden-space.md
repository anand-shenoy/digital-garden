# Bifold Panelling to Hide Utility and a Guest Loo in Circulation Space

**Type:** Design solution
**Project:** Queen's Road

## The Constraint
A lot of existing utility area was being wasted purely as circulation space, with no other function.

## The Idea
Created bifold panelling that closes to hide the utility area, and added a guest loo in the middle of the house behind the same treatment. From the kitchen side, the panelling just reads as a decorative wall, guests walking past it to reach the kitchen have no sense that utility and a loo are sitting right behind it.

## Why It Worked
Circulation space is normally treated as dead space to minimise, not a place to hide function. Here the circulation itself became the disguise.
