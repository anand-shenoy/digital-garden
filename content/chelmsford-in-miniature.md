# Chelmsford in miniature

**Type:** Interactive model
**Status:** Live, version 9

The middle of Chelmsford as a clay architectural model you can hold in your hand.
Every building comes from OpenStreetMap, the light follows the time of day, and the
trains run to the real timetable.

You arrive by dropping down through the clouds, and the town settles into a round
disc that fades to white at the edge, like a model on a table.

Full screen is the better way to see it, on a phone as much as a desktop. Drag to
look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap anything.

## What is in it

| Layer | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Trains | 3D trains on the raised line, timed to the Greater Anglia timetable, with a departures board |
| Events | What is on this week at the theatre, the cathedral and the market, each checked on the venue's own page |
| Places | Shops, cafes, parks and public buildings, with a pin you can tap |
| Walk | Tap the ground to see 5, 10 and 15 minute walking rings along real paths, and the way to the nearest places |

The time slider moves the sun. After dusk the windows light up floor by floor and
the street lamps come on.

## Landmarks you will recognise

Zoom in on the cathedral and its west tower gains pinnacles, the octagonal lantern
and the needle spire. Shire Hall shows its stone front looking down the High Street,
with the pediment on four columns and the three arched entrances. Judge Tindal
stands in Tindal Square, and the railway runs on its brick arches and embankment,
just as it does.

These details only appear as you get close, so the model stays light from a
distance and familiar up close.

## Known limits

The timetable is a typical weekday, Saturday and Sunday pattern, not today's live
running. The events are for the week of 22 October 2026 and will go out of date.
Chelmsford Museum at Oaklands Park sits just outside the disc, so it is not in the
model.
