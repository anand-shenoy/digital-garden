# Sketch-to-Render Hybrid for Marketing

**Type:** Marketing idea / technique

## The Idea
A single image split in two: one half a hand-drawn concept sketch, the other half a photoreal render or photo of the same view. Used as social content to make the design process itself visible.

## Why It Works
Shows the "sketch to real" story in one frame, more compelling as marketing content than either a sketch or a render alone. Works for Studio ASD and any architecture studio's marketing/social content, not tied to a specific project.

## How to Produce It
Could be done as a manual illustration overlay, or via AI image generation, feeding in a real photo/render and generating the sketch half as a composite.
